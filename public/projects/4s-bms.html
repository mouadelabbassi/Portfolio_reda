Featured engineering project / Battery management

4S Lithium-Ion Battery Management System

PCB design, battery simulation, and an interactive monitoring dashboard.

Design and simulation completed · Hardware implementation pending

An academic engineering project combining an ESP32- and BQ76942-based board design, MATLAB/Simulink battery modeling, and a browser-based BMS Cockpit. The work studies four lithium-ion cell groups in series, condition monitoring, modeled protection, and passive cell balancing. The cockpit replays simulation exports—not live hardware data.

01 / PURPOSE

Objective

Study how a battery management system monitors a series-connected lithium-ion pack, identifies abnormal operating conditions, and addresses imbalance between groups. The project connects three engineering layers: PCB design, dynamic simulation, and visual analysis of exported data.

What the system studies

Per-group voltage, current, temperature and state of charge, alongside passive balancing and protective fault behavior.

What is verified today

A completed design-and-simulation workflow. There is no claim of assembled hardware, firmware operation or physical test performance.

02 / KICAD

Hardware design

A KiCad schematic, PCB layout and 3D board model were developed around the ESP32 and BQ76942. The design addresses sensing, balancing, protection switching and power routing for a 4S, 16.8 V maximum pack.

Sensing & control

Cell-group measurement connections, current-sensing shunt and temperature-sensing inputs; ESP32 and BQ76942 integration in the board design.

Power & protection

Protection MOSFETs and passive-balancing circuitry, with power-routing calculations oriented toward the 10 A design target.

Engineering boundary: PCB layout and power-routing calculations are design work. Copper heating, connector limits, protection switching, measurement accuracy and current capability still require bench validation.
03 / MATLAB + SIMULINK

Modeling & simulation

The MATLAB/Simulink model represents individual series-group voltages, state of charge and temperature, plus passive cell balancing. It also models latched responses to undervoltage, overvoltage, overcurrent and overtemperature conditions.

Battery states

Group voltage, SOC and temperature evolve with charging, discharging and unequal group conditions.

Protection logic

Undervoltage (UV), overvoltage (OV), overcurrent (OC) and overtemperature (OT) are modeled with latched fault states.

The modeled protections are simulation behavior. They do not demonstrate implemented ESP32 firmware, configured BQ76942 protection thresholds, or physical actuation.

04 / MODEL SCENARIOS

Simulation scenarios

The simulation explores six operating and fault scenarios. The labels below describe the engineering questions investigated; they are not measured acceptance results.

Normal operation

Observe pack and group measurements during nominal modeled conditions.

Discharge

Trace SOC and group voltages as modeled charge is depleted.

Charging

Observe voltage and SOC evolution during simulated charging.

Cell imbalance

Study unequal group states and the passive-balancing response.

Overcurrent

Inspect current excursion and modeled latched protection state.

Overtemperature

Inspect temperature excursion and modeled latched protection state.

05 / BROWSER DASHBOARD

BMS Cockpit

A responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript dashboard for exploring battery simulation output. It imports JSON exported from MATLAB, then replays simulation samples in the browser so engineering behavior can be inspected visually.

Import & playback

Load MATLAB JSON exports and play back simulation timelines.

Battery indicators

Track simulated cell-group and pack information during replay.

Fault visualization

Inspect modeled protection and warning states.

Charts & capture

Review trends with analysis charts and export a PNG capture.

Data provenance: dashboard values come from simulation JSON exports. There is no live ESP32 communication, live sensor acquisition or connected physical BMS at this stage.
